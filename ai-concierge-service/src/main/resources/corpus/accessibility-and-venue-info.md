# SeatSync Accessibility and Venue Information

SeatSync is committed to making events usable by everyone. This document
explains what accessibility support exists, how to request it, and where to
find venue information.

## Finding venue information

Every event page shows its venue's name, city and street address. Seat maps
are organized by section, row and seat number (seat IDs look like A-3-12:
section A, row 3, seat 12). Row 1 of each section is the row closest to the
stage or floor level and involves the fewest steps to reach. For live venue
details on a specific event, ask the concierge or open the event page — the
data comes straight from the event catalog.

## Wheelchair users and step-free access

Wheelchair spaces are physical arrangements made by the venue, not a separate
seat type on the seat map. To arrange one:

1. Book any seat in **row 1** of the section you prefer (row 1 is the
   step-free row at all SeatSync-listed venues).
2. Email **support@seatsync.local** with the subject line **"Accessibility"**,
   your booking ID and what you need, **at least 72 hours before the event**.
3. The SeatSync accessibility team coordinates with the venue and confirms by
   email, at no extra charge. If your preferred section's row 1 is sold out,
   the team will relocate you to an equivalent accessible position without a
   price difference.

## Companion seating

If you need a companion or care assistant seated next to you, hold and book
two adjacent seats in the same transaction window (each seat gets its own
5-minute hold), then mention both booking IDs in your accessibility email so
the venue treats them as one party. Companion tickets are charged at the
normal seat price.

## Assistive listening and hearing support

Assistive listening devices and hearing-loop positions can be requested for
any event with the same process: email support@seatsync.local, subject
"Accessibility", at least 72 hours ahead. Availability of specific equipment
depends on the venue, and the team always answers with what is possible
before the event.

## Service animals

Trained service animals are welcome at every venue listed on SeatSync at no
charge. Please still mention the animal in an accessibility email so the venue
can seat you with adequate space (aisle-adjacent positions in row 1 are
usually recommended).

## If something goes wrong

If a properly requested accessibility arrangement (made at least 72 hours in
advance) was not provided at the event, you are entitled to a **full 100%
refund regardless of timing** — see the Refund and Cancellation Policy. Report
it to support@seatsync.local within 30 days of the event with your booking ID.

## Questions the concierge cannot answer

Details that depend on the individual venue building — parking spaces, exact
elevator locations, accessible restroom locations — are not stored in
SeatSync. For those, email support@seatsync.local and a human will check with
the venue directly.
