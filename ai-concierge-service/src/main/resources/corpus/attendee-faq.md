# SeatSync Attendee FAQ

## Do I need an account to browse events?

No. Searching events and watching live seat maps works without an account.
You only need to sign in (as an ATTENDEE) to hold a seat, book it, or join a
waitlist.

## How do I find events?

Search by free text (event name or description), filter by city, or filter by
category — CONCERT, WORKSHOP, SPORTS, CAMPUS or OTHER. Every event page shows
the venue, its address, the start time in UTC and the price range. The
concierge can also look up current events, event details and live seat
availability for you.

## What do the seat colors mean?

The seat map updates live for everyone:

- **Available** seats are shown in the outlined/primary style and can be
  clicked to hold.
- **Held by you** seats are highlighted with your personal countdown timer.
- **Held by someone else** seats are greyed out and disabled.
- **Booked** seats are dark and disabled.

## How long do I have to complete a purchase?

Clicking a seat gives you an exclusive **5-minute hold**. Confirm within those
5 minutes to pay and get the seat; otherwise the hold expires automatically
and the seat goes back on sale (or to the waitlist if there is one). Holds are
free — you pay only when you confirm.

## Can I buy several seats for a group?

Yes. Hold each seat you want — you can have several active holds at once, each
with its own 5-minute timer — then confirm each hold. Seats are identified by
section-row-number (for example A-3-12), so pick adjacent numbers in the same
row to sit together.

## Why did I get "seat is already held" or "Seat was just taken"?

"Seat is already held" means someone beat you to the hold — only one active
hold can exist per seat. "Seat was just taken" is rarer: it means the final
availability check at confirmation found a conflict on that seat. In both
cases you were not charged; pick another seat.

## What is the waitlist and how does it work?

When an event is sold out you can join its waitlist (free, one entry per
person per event). As soon as a seat frees up it is offered by email to the
person who has waited the longest, and the offer stays valid for **10
minutes**. Miss the window and the seat moves to the next person; you keep
nothing to pay if you never accept an offer.

## How do I get my ticket?

After confirming, you receive a confirmation email with your booking ID, the
event name and your exact seat ID. Your bookings are also listed on the
"My Bookings" page. Show the booking confirmation at the venue entrance.

## Can I cancel and get my money back?

Yes, from "My Bookings", subject to the refund policy: **100% refund if you
cancel at least 48 hours before the event starts, 50% within the last 48
hours, and no refund once the event has started** (no-shows included). If the
organizer cancels the event you always get 100% back. Refunds return to your
original payment method within 5–10 business days.

## Can I change my seat instead of cancelling?

There is no direct seat-swap. Hold the new seat first, confirm it, then cancel
the old booking — mind the refund windows above, since the cancellation is a
normal cancellation.

## I need accessible seating — what do I do?

Book a row 1 seat and email support@seatsync.local with subject
"Accessibility" at least 72 hours before the event. See the Accessibility and
Venue Information guide for the full process, companion seating and service
animal rules.

## Something else went wrong. Who do I contact?

Email **support@seatsync.local** with your booking ID (if you have one). A
human reads every message. For payment disputes, write within 30 days of the
charge.
