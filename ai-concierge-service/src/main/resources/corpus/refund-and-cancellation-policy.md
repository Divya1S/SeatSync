# SeatSync Refund and Cancellation Policy

This policy applies to every ticket booked on SeatSync, regardless of event
category (CONCERT, WORKSHOP, SPORTS, CAMPUS or OTHER) or price tier
(STANDARD, PREMIUM or VIP). SeatSync charges no service or booking fees: the
price shown on the seat map is the full price you pay, and it is the amount
used to calculate any refund.

## Cancelling a booking yourself

You can cancel any confirmed booking from the "My Bookings" page. The refund
amount depends on when you cancel, measured against the event's scheduled
start time (shown in UTC on the event page):

- **48 hours or more before the event starts: 100% refund.**
- **Less than 48 hours before the event starts: 50% refund.**
- **After the event has started: no refund.** This includes no-shows.

Cancellation takes effect immediately. Your seat returns to the open pool
right away and, if the event has a waitlist, the seat is automatically offered
to the person who has been waiting the longest.

## How refunds are paid

Refunds always go back to the original payment method used for the booking.
Processing takes 5 to 10 business days from the moment the cancellation is
confirmed. SeatSync never issues refunds as vouchers or credit unless you
explicitly request it.

## Events cancelled by the organizer

If an organizer cancels an event, every confirmed booking for that event is
refunded at **100%, regardless of timing**, including bookings made minutes
before the cancellation. You will receive a cancellation email, and the refund
follows the same 5–10 business day schedule. You do not need to do anything to
claim this refund.

## Holds and the waitlist are free

Placing a 5-minute hold on a seat costs nothing — payment happens only when
you confirm the hold into a booking. If a hold expires or you release it, no
money ever changes hands. Joining an event's waitlist is also completely free.

## Accessibility-related refunds

If you booked a seat because of an accessibility arrangement and the venue
failed to provide it (for example a wheelchair space or an assistive listening
device that was requested at least 72 hours in advance), you are entitled to a
**100% refund regardless of when you report it**, up to 30 days after the
event. Email support@seatsync.local with the booking ID.

## Disputes and duplicate charges

If you believe you were charged twice, or a refund has not arrived after 10
business days, email support@seatsync.local with your booking ID (shown in
your confirmation email) within 30 days of the charge. A human reviews every
dispute; there is no automated rejection.
